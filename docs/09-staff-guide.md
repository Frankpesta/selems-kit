# How to use the Selems admin

For front office and management. Sign in at **selemsexclusivehotel.com/admin** with the email the manager invited. Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page; a code is emailed to you.

**Admin** can do everything. **Staff** can do reservations, availability, inquiries, check-in and check-out.

Search for any guest from any screen: press **Ctrl + K** and type a name, phone number or booking reference (SLM-26-0424).

---

## 1. Take a booking by phone, WhatsApp or walk-in

1. **Reservations** → **New booking**.
2. Choose the dates and number of guests. The list shows only room types that are free, with how many are left.
3. Pick the room type (and a specific room number if the guest asks).
4. Enter the guest's name and phone. Email is optional for walk-ins, but add it if you can, because the guest then gets a confirmation email.
5. Check the price shown, then create the booking. The room is held immediately; nobody online can take it.

To change the price for this guest, enter a different rate. You must give a reason; it is recorded.

## 2. Check a guest in

1. **Today** → **Arrivals today** → **Check in** on the guest's row.
   (Or open the booking and press **Check in**.)
2. If the guest pays now, press **Record payment** on the booking: amount, method (cash, transfer, POS), note.

Late arrivals after the cutoff time (Settings, default 8 pm) show under **Needs attention**. Call the guest before you mark a **no-show**.

## 3. Check a guest out

1. **Today** → **Departures today** → **Check out**.
2. If there is a balance, record the payment first. Checking out with money still owed asks for a reason.

## 4. Move, change or cancel a booking

Open the booking (from **Reservations**, **Today** or Ctrl + K):

- **Change dates**: the system checks the room is free and shows the new price.
- **Move room**: pick another free room. On **Availability** you can also drag the booking to another room's row.
- **Cancel booking**: the screen tells you exactly what happens (the room is freed; the guest gets an email).
- **Resend confirmation** if the guest didn't receive the email.

If a room is already taken, the system refuses and tells you why. Nothing is double-booked.

## 5. Change a room price (Admin)

**Everyday price:** **Rooms & rates** → click the room type → change the weekday or weekend rate → **Save changes**. Weekend means Friday and Saturday nights. You can type `70000`, `70,000` or `₦70,000`.

**Price for particular dates** (Christmas, convocation weekend): on the same page, under **Special-date rates**, **Add date range**: first night, last night, rate, a label → **Save rate**.

The website and the booking form show the new price within seconds. Existing bookings keep the price they were booked at.

## 6. Add an offer (Admin)

**Offers** → **New offer**: title, short summary, photo, number of nights, total price, which room types, which arrival days, the dates it runs, terms. Tick **Show on the Home page** to feature it, and **Published** to make it live. An offer shows as Scheduled until its start date and disappears after it ends.

## 7. Upload photos (Admin)

**Gallery** → choose the category next to **Upload as** → **Upload photos** → select several at once.

Every photo needs **alt text**: one sentence describing what is in the photo, e.g. *"VIP Suite bedroom with a king bed and city view, Selems Hotel, Akure"*. Google reads it and so do blind guests' screen readers. **A photo cannot be published without it.**

Use real photos only, landscape, at least 1200 pixels wide. Phone photos are fine if they are sharp and well lit.

To add photos to a room page: **Rooms & rates** → the room type → photos. The first photo is the cover.

## 8. Website text (Admin)

**Content & SEO** → choose the page. Edit, then **Preview** to see it on the real page before anyone else does. **Publish changes** makes it live. The panel on the right warns you if the Google title or description is too long, or if anything is missing.

## 9. Messages from the website

**Inquiries** shows contact-form messages. Reply by phone, WhatsApp or email, then **Mark replied**. **Create booking from this** fills in the new-booking form for you.

## Need help?

Every change is recorded with who made it and when (**Audit log**, Admin). If something looks wrong, don't try to fix it twice: note the booking reference and tell the manager.
