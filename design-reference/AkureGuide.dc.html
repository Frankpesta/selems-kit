Akure at a glance

Akure is the capital of Ondo State in south-west Nigeria. Most visitors come for government business, the Federal University of Technology (FUTA), weddings and family events, or as a base for Idanre Hills. The city is compact, so where you stay mostly decides how quiet your nights are and how long your morning drive takes.

Best areas to stay in Akure

Alagbaka

The government district. Close to the State Secretariat and ministries; best for official visits.

Ijapo Estate and Extension

Residential and quiet, with good road access across the city. Selems is here.

Oba-Ile

Near Akure Airport. Handy for early flights.

FUTA axis

For convocations, conferences and campus visits.

What to see nearby

Idanre Hills, a short drive from the city, is the best-known sight in the state: a climb of several hundred steps to an old hilltop settlement with wide views. In town, the Deji of Akure's palace and the Oja Oba market beside it are worth an afternoon.
