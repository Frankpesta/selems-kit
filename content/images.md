# Photo manifest

19 real property photos taken from the current website (2024 shoot), re-encoded at max 2400px wide (JPEG q84), plus the logo. Stock images used on the old site (men in suits, Wi-Fi sign, pool breakfast, TV render) were deliberately **not** carried over.

All files live in `public/images/` and are referenced from `content/seed.json` as `/images/<file>`. The room ↔ photo mapping is **provisional**: the old site reused the same few photos across several room types. Confirm with the hotel which photo shows which room, and schedule a shoot for the missing ones.
